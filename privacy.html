Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

"Byte Brief Uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the GeeKShoo YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos.

What the App accesses

Through Google OAuth, the App requests permission to upload videos and to manage captions and thumbnails on the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl).

How data is used

  • The App only uploads videos, captions and thumbnails that the channel owner produced, and reads basic channel information to confirm the target channel.
  • The App does not collect, sell or share personal data of any user or viewer.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never transmitted to third parties.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
